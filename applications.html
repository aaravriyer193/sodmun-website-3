Questions

Frequently
asked.

What is SODMUN?

SODMUN stands for Summit of Diplomacy Model United Nations. It is a teen-owned Model UN conference in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, run entirely by students, with more than 1,500 delegates across its first three editions.

Where and when does SODMUN take place?

SODMUN takes place in Dubai, UAE, over three days. SODMUN V begins on 11 December 2026; the venue will be announced on this site and on Instagram @sod.mun.

Who can take part in SODMUN?

Any secondary school student can apply, whether it is your first conference or your fiftieth. Students can apply individually or as a school delegation, and experienced MUNers can apply to chair.

How many committees does SODMUN have?

SODMUN has 25 committees: traditional councils such as the Security Council, GA1, GA2, GA4, UNHRC, WHO, UNICEF and UNEP; crisis committees such as Formula 1, FIFA, INTERPOL and The Black Chamber; and specialised committees including the ICJ, ICAO and the International Press Corps.

Do I need experience to be a SODMUN delegate?

No. Committees are designed for a range of experience levels, and background guides and delegate resources are published before the conference to help first-time delegates prepare.

Does SODMUN use a conference platform?

Yes. Since SODMUN IV, committees run on SODMUN’s own platform at app.sodmun.com, with delegate chat, bloc messaging for points of information, an AI assistant and a speaking-list timer, so committees run paperless.
