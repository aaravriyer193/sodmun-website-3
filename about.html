Summit of Diplomacy Model United Nations is a real-world simulation of international diplomacy and global governance, run entirely by students in Dubai.

Our story

SODMUN began with a simple idea: build the conference we always wished we could attend, with committees that are rigorous, creative and fun. Across SODMUN I, II and III, more than 1,500 students took their seats in committee. SODMUN IV brought together over 700 delegates across 25 committees, from the Security Council to Formula 1. Whether you want to sharpen your diplomacy, find confidence at the podium or meet the people you will build the future with, SODMUN is built to be the most memorable MUN you attend.
